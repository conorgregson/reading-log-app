Backup Types

Backup data exports your full library:

  • Profile (daily goal + book goals)
  • Books (status, series, formats, ISBN, etc.)
  • Reading sessions (your log history)

Use Sessions backup if you only need to move or merge reading sessions between devices - it won't touch your books or profile.

Sessions backup

Sessions backup exports only your reading sessions (dates, minutes, pages, and notes) and merges them into your log without changing your books or goals.

Use up and down arrows to navigate. Press Enter to activate, Escape to close.
